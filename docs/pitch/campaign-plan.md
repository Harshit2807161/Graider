Status: full plan, 2026-10-12, replacing the checkpoint-1 outline. Week 1 runs from Tuesday 13 October 2026; week 8 ends Sunday 6 December. Any number without a source is a target. Materials (in `materials/`) and lists (`research/target-lists.md`) are referred to, not copied.

## 1. Objective for week eight

Targets for 6 December 2026: 7 pilots agreed (4 coaching centres, 3 schools), 5 of them running or finished on a live test, 2 coaching pilots converted to paid use and 2 schools with a written place in their February to March budget discussion, 3 professor comparisons finished and 1 written up with permission, and 6 first investor conversations held.

## 2. Why this order

**The budget-window correction.** The brief's "budget window is open now" did not survive checking. The one source, RAYSolute (a consultancy; figures unsourced), says schools set budgets in February to March and "Don't expect sales in Dec-Jan or Aug-Nov"; cycles run 4 to 9 months; the principal is the gatekeeper and a trustee or director approves larger spend (`research/claims.md` §5). School pilots now aim at the February to March decision; no school revenue is planned in these eight weeks.

**Segments, in order** (`research/target-lists.md` §0 and §2):

| Order | Segment | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Owner-led coaching: CBSE board prep, CA/CS/CMA, UPSC answer writing | Owner decides (inferred, unverified); frequent handwritten tests; no school budget cycle. The conversion target sits here |
| 2 | Mid-market CBSE schools, in parallel | Principal-led at small schools; a November to January pilot on a unit test or pre-board feeds the February to March decision. Board exams start mid-February (background knowledge), so pilot early |
| 3 | College departments, through one professor | No DU college is UGC-autonomous, but DU teachers set and mark internal class tests, so one teacher can run a test without the exam cell. Produces the method write-up |
| 4 | Premium and chain schools | Society or chairman sign-off; reputation-sensitive after CBSE 2026. Only once references exist |
| 5 | NEET/JEE Main brands | Tests are mostly MCQ, OMR or computer-based. Skip, except foundation or JEE Advanced written tests |

**Investors last.** Inc42 reported Indian edtech funding fell to $249M across 31 deals in 2025, an eight-year low (Inc42, 15 Jan 2026; `research/claims.md` §3). The pitch, vertical AI for assessment with a teacher in the loop, needs running pilots. Y Combinator has backed Edexia (W25) and Frizzle (S25) (`research/claims.md` §4); nothing public says either raised on pilots alone, so we don't say it.

## 4. How to build the lists

Method in full: `research/target-lists.md` §1. About one working day per segment.

- **Coaching:** Google Maps by locality (Old Rajinder Nagar, Mukherjee Nagar, Karol Bagh for UPSC; Laxmi Nagar, Janakpuri, Rohini, Dwarka, Kalkaji for CBSE and CA/CS), then Justdial by hand, then each institute's test-series and careers pages (an evaluator opening means handwritten volume).
- **Schools:** CBSE SARAS directory by district (Delhi, Gurugram, Gautam Buddh Nagar, Ghaziabad, Faridabad), since mid-market schools rarely have a findable website; school sites for datesheets, ERP logins and leadership titles. Chains: one campus, never the group.
- **Professors:** by department, not autonomy: a notice or ordinance showing written internal tests (management, law, commerce, humanities). Approach the teacher who owns the paper, not the exam cell.
- **Every row:** source, date checked, `verified-on-site / listing-only / unverified`; re-check after 90 days. Titles and public channels only; names go in the private sheet.

## 5. Outreach sequence

Kirtan sends every message himself, from his own address.

| Audience | Messages | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| Coaching-centre head | `materials/messages.md`, "Coaching-centre head" | Enquiry form or generic email, then a call; offer a day and time |
| Principal or trustee | "Principal or trustee" | School email to the principal's office, then a call asking who in management sees technology pilots |
| Professor | "Professor" | Institutional email to the teacher who owns the paper |
| Investor | "Investor" | Warm introduction only; no deck until they reply |

1. **First contact**, from the file above, personalised as it says.
2. **Follow-up** at one week, same file.
3. **Second follow-up** at three weeks: "Dear [Name], one last note on Gr8r. Here is a real handwritten answer marked by it, with the evidence for each mark: [site]/sample. If a pilot on one internal exam is ever useful, reply and I'll set it up. Regards, Kirtan".
4. **Stop.** Mark the row `stopped`. No further message in these eight weeks.

**The 20-minute demo**, on a laptop running Gr8r:

1. **Their sheet (2 min).** Ask two days ahead for one recent answer sheet, unmarked if possible.
2. **Their marking scheme (3 min).** Entered the day before (typed or from Excel). Show the criteria and have them confirm one.
3. **Upload (2 min).** Upload their photos or PDF live; explain the second examiner while it runs. Keep a copy marked earlier in case the network fails.
4. **The review screen (5 min).** One answer: page beside transcript, each criterion's mark with its highlighted quote, the second examiner's view, any escalation and its reason.
5. **The one wrong mark (4 min).** A mark they disagree with or an escalated answer (if none, Q35(a) from the CBSE run). They override it with a reason; show the change recorded and approval waiting until the queue is clear.
6. **The pilot offer (4 min).** Hand over `materials/pilot-offer.md`, fill in exam, class and start date, and book the kick-off within a week. Objections: `materials/objections.md`.

## 6. Pilot playbook

**Kick-off checklist** (week 1 of the pilot):
- `materials/pilot-offer.md` signed, every blank filled, conversion date included.
- A named contact and a reviewing teacher, with logins.
- Marking scheme entered; the teacher confirms every question's criteria.
- Questions expecting a drawing listed; the teacher marks those.
- Five sheets uploaded first to test scan quality.
- The teacher times their own marking of ten sheets, scanned before marking (criterion 2).
- An English-medium internal exam; no board exam.

**Success criteria, written per pilot** (from `materials/pilot-offer.md`, with how each is measured):

| Criterion | Target | Measured from |
|---|---|---|
| Marks accepted unchanged, among answers not escalated | ___% | Overrides in the submission timeline over answers not escalated |
| Teacher's time per sheet, review included | under ___ min | Teacher's log, against the ten hand-marked sheets |
| Every escalated answer reviewed | all | Review queue empty (approval is refused until it is) |
| Other | ___ | ___ |

**Weekly check-in** (15 minutes, fixed day, with the contact): sheets uploaded, marked, reviewed, approved; marks changed and why; escalations by reason; teacher time; problems, each logged in `docs/v2-backlog.md`; next week's actions.

**End-of-pilot report:** exam and setup; each criterion, met or not, with number and method; what changed and why; what was escalated and why; time per sheet; what went wrong and our fix; the decision asked for.

**Conversion conversation** (on the conversion date, with whoever decides: the owner, or the principal and management): walk through the report and ask for a written decision. Coaching: continue on a paid per-sheet basis at a rate agreed then, or stop. Schools: continue paid, or a dated note placing Gr8r in the February to March budget discussion with its approver named, or stop. No free extensions. Then ask for reference consent (none, name, quote or logo).

## 7. What to measure weekly

Every Sunday, 30 minutes, the three founders, from the sheet:

- **Funnel per segment:** listed, contacted, FU, FU2, replied (warm and cold apart), demo, pilot agreed, running, reported, converted, stopped.
- **Per pilot:** sheets marked, marks accepted unchanged, escalation rate and reasons, time per sheet, changed marks that were not escalated, model cost per sheet (from recorded calls).
- **Professors:** sheets compared, agreement per criterion, permission to write up.
- **Investors:** listed, introductions asked, first conversations, second meetings.
- **Against section 3's targets.** A segment behind two weeks running gives its next week's hours to one that is ahead.

## 8. When the site changes

- **Launch (week 1):** no numbers, no names.
- **After the hand-marked CBSE comparison (weeks 4 to 5):** `/results`, with method, sample size and caveats beside every figure, only if the method holds.
- **After each pilot report (weeks 7 and 8):** the honest section updated with what pilots showed.
- **References:** a name, quote or logo only with written consent, recorded in `consent_for_reference`.
- **Never:** a figure without its method beside it.

## 9. Risks and what to do

| Risk | What to do |
|---|---|
| The CBSE 2026 episode comes up | Don't raise it first. Answer from `materials/objections.md`, keeping to the confirmed facts (`research/claims.md` §1): Class 12 board marking on screen; CBSE said its vendor's portal vulnerabilities were contained (31 May 2026); over 13,000 hard-to-read sheets were checked by hand (Education Secretary); a parliamentary committee invited, not summoned, CBSE's chairman; only press reports quote CBSE saying AI was not used. Then turn to internal exams, scan checks and teacher approval |
| A pilot school wants board-exam marking | Decline in writing; the pilot offer excludes it. Offer the pre-board instead |
| A pilot exposes a product gap (diagrams) | A missed drawing can score zero unflagged today (`docs/v2-backlog.md`). Drawing questions go to the teacher from kick-off; the report records the gap; no fix date promised; prefer exams light on drawings |
| A teacher won't use the review queue | Sit with them for their first ten sheets. If nothing is reviewed a week later, ask the contact for another teacher. Never review or approve for them |
| No replies in week two | Change the channel, not the volume: phone the centre or school office, walk the coaching clusters with a printed sample, ask the warm contacts for one introduction each. Rewrite the first line only after 20 cold messages with no reply |
