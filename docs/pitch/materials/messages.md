# Outreach messages

Kirtan sends every message himself. Each first contact asks for 20 minutes and offers to mark one of the reader's own answer sheets live. Each follow-up goes one week later if there is no reply. Text in [brackets] is filled in per person.

## Principal or trustee

**First contact**

> Dear [Name], I'm Kirtan Jain, co-founder of Gr8r, a small team in Delhi. Gr8r marks handwritten answer sheets for internal exams, such as unit tests and pre-boards, against your school's own marking scheme. Every mark comes with a quote from the student's answer as its evidence, a second model checks each mark, and anything it can't settle goes to your teacher. No result reaches a student until a teacher approves it. We don't do board-exam marking. Could I have 20 minutes with you or your exam coordinator? Share one answer sheet from a recent test with its marking scheme, and I'll mark it live in front of you. Regards, Kirtan Jain, Gr8r, [phone]

**Follow-up**

> Dear [Name], following up on my note last week about Gr8r. The offer stands: 20 minutes, one of your own answer sheets and its marking scheme, marked live, with the evidence for every mark on screen. If it looks useful, we can run a free four-week pilot on one exam for one class, with success criteria agreed in writing first. Would [day] or [day] suit you? Regards, Kirtan

**Personalising:** name the test they are in the middle of (unit test, half-yearly, pre-board) and the person who introduced you, if anyone. For a trustee, lead with teacher sign-off before results go out; for a principal, lead with the marking scheme being their own.

**Follow-up**

> Hi [Name], checking back on Gr8r. Still happy to mark one of your mock-test sheets live in 20 minutes, with every mark's evidence on screen. If it works for you, we run one mock test for one batch free for four weeks, with the targets written down before we start. Does [day] work? Kirtan

**Personalising:** use the centre's subjects and exam (JEE, NEET, board preparation) and the size of a typical batch if you know it. Coaching heads decide quickly, so offer a specific day and time in the first message.

## Professor

**First contact**

> Dear Prof. [Name], I'm Kirtan Jain, co-founder of Gr8r. We mark handwritten answer sheets with AI, and I'd value your view on our method. Each mark cites a criterion from your rubric and a quote from the student's writing, and code checks that the quote is really in the transcript. A second, different model scores the answer itself before it reviews the first grade, and disagreements beyond a threshold go to you. We haven't measured agreement with teachers yet, which is why I'm writing: would you compare its marks with yours on one test in one course? Could we start with 20 minutes, with one answer sheet and your rubric, marked live? Regards, Kirtan Jain

**Follow-up**

> Dear Prof. [Name], following up on Gr8r. The proposal is small: one test in one course, your rubric, your marks beside ours, and the full reasoning for every mark open to you, including where the two models disagreed. It can run on API models or on open-weight models you host. Twenty minutes to start, marking one of your sheets live? Regards, Kirtan

**Personalising:** name the course and the kind of internal test it has (written, long answers, numericals). If they work on assessment, AI or education research, say so and offer them the method write-up from the comparison.
