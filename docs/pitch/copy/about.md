## Section 1. Why we built it

Heading: Why we built it

Body:
Unit tests, pre-boards, mock tests and internal assessments are written by hand and marked by hand, usually by the teachers who teach the class. Current models can read neat handwriting and follow a marking scheme. But if a teacher can't see why a model gave a mark, they have to mark the answer again anyway, and a student or parent has no reason to accept it.

So we built Gr8r around the habit every student is taught: show your working. Every mark refers to an item in the marking scheme and quotes the student's own words. A second model checks it. What the system can't settle goes to the teacher, and nothing reaches a student until a teacher approves it. We also say plainly what it can't do yet, on every page where it matters.

## Section 3. How we work

Heading: How we work

- Our sample is a real answer book, and it shows the mistakes Gr8r made on it.
- There is no accuracy figure on this site, because we haven't measured one against teachers' marks yet. When we have one, we'll publish the method and every case with it.
- Your teachers approve every result, and we delete your data when you ask.
- We work on internal exams only. We don't mark board exams.
